## Compression evidence

The initial 448 tracked project-owned Markdown files were screened in 18 bounded
batches of at most 25 files, with current authority reviewed before historical prose.
Counts exclude fenced/indented code and tables. At the semantic checkpoint the corpus
contained 257,519 words; the compression pass retained 257,515. Only two occurrences
of `as well as` became `and`: feature 026 research and feature 019 dated verification.
All other files have an explicit KEEP decision. No requirement/task identifier, command,
table, date, result, caveat or evidence identity was removed.

This was a conservative redundancy screen with candidate inspection, not a
sentence-by-sentence semantic review of every historical document. Similar historical
prose can record different evidence scopes, so uncertain duplication remains. There
was no percentage target. Authority moves and meaning corrections are not counted as
lossless phrase reductions. The later feature 031 navigation correction below is also
a semantic amendment, outside that checkpoint.

The ignored task evidence directory `.tmp/correctness-docs-20261015/` retains
`baseline/`, `precompression/`, `semantic-checkpoint/`, `counts.csv`, `movements.json`,
`removal-log.md` and `compression-scorecard.md`. The counts include initial, semantic
and compressed word counts, per-file SHA256 identities and KEEP/REMOVE decisions.
Per-run compression outputs remain outside version control under the compression
skill's save policy; the maintained documents are the edited originals.
