Spec Kit is temporarily disabled. Skills were moved here intact from `.agents/skills`
to remove them from Codex and Claude discovery. The existing `.specify/` and `specs/`
trees are also frozen archives; do not run their workflows or update their artifacts.

[workflow policy](../../docs/agent-workflow.md). Product requirements remain binding;
use [current contracts](../../docs/current-contracts.md). Historical sources may be
read for provenance, but their workflow instructions are inactive.
