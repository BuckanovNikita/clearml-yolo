# ADR-0201 · Train and predict with Ultralytics YOLO

The default detector uses the installed Ultralytics trainer and predictor. CY passes stage-specific native settings and forces the inputs needed for detection on the prepared ground truth. The native detection model must be explicit in the effective settings. Its best checkpoint is `weights/best.pt`.

Prediction maps the native detections into original-image coordinates for the shared prediction workflow. A changed detector-family setting in an inactive group fails validation. The native training path propagates resource and numerical failures without trying another batch, precision or compiler configuration.
